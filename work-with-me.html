For teachers

Evidence-based AI literacy workshops

My core format is a 90-minute, hands-on workshop for whole school staff, the format tested in my own intervention research. Teachers leave with practice, not slides.

What teachers work on

  • What generative AI is, how it works, and what that means for teaching
  • Practical and ethical uses in lesson planning and assessment, done hands-on
  • Verifying AI output: spotting errors, bias and unsupported claims before they reach students
  • How students are already using AI, and how to adapt tasks and assessment

How it works

  1. A short conversation about your staff, context and goals.
  2. An optional readiness snapshot using the aittt.app research instrument, so the session meets teachers where they are.
  3. The workshop, tailored to your context.
  4. Follow-up practice through the SCALE toolkit.
  5. Optional measurement, so you can see what changed, and, where appropriate and with consent, contribute anonymously to research.

Formats: whole-staff workshop of 90 minutes. Full-day programme, which is where the verification and assessment work really lands. A short series across a term. In person or online, and the online version is the same session, not a watered down one.

Annual pro bono programme. Each year I offer free AI literacy workshops to selected schools in India, prioritising schools that would not otherwise have access to this kind of professional development.

Keynotes and talks

Talks with substance

I speak to teachers, school leaders, teacher educators and policymakers. My talks go beyond prompting tips to what the evidence says and what it means for practice.

Sample talk titles

  • Beyond prompting: what teachers really need to know about AI
  • Classical, generative, agentic: what changes for teaching when AI starts to act
  • Why teachers adopt AI, or don't: lessons from nearly 16,000 teachers in 19 countries
  • Do AI tutors actually teach? What the evidence says about scaffolding
  • From compliance to classroom: AI governance schools can live with
  • Judgement, not prompts: building AI literacy that lasts

Formats: keynote, panel, or keynote with live demonstration.

For leaders and systems

Advice grounded in research

I advise school leaders, school groups, universities and education authorities on:

  • AI literacy strategy and professional development design, using the GenAI-TPD framework to plan staged, measurable teacher development
  • Diagnosing readiness before investing in training
  • AI governance and policy, including high-risk uses, data protection and explainability, with reference to UNESCO and OECD guidance and the EU AI Act
  • AI credentials and curricula in higher education, using the AI Credential Maturity Model
  • Evaluating AI tutoring tools against pedagogical principles such as contingent scaffolding

Research partnership

Take part in research

I am looking for schools, school networks, universities and education systems to take part in studies on teacher AI literacy, especially in India and Finland, and increasingly beyond.

What partners receive

  • Evidence about their own context, shared back in an accessible form
  • A workshop for participating staff
  • Early access to findings
  • Where appropriate, recognition in publications

What I bring

  • Rigorous methods: systematic review, meta-analysis, structural equation modelling, field interventions and blind double coding
  • Anonymous data collection with informed consent, following Finnish research integrity guidelines and university data protection requirements
  • Cross-national comparison that places your context alongside others

Studies currently open to partners: teacher deference to AI judgements (schools in India); AI adoption in Finnish high schools (schools in Finland); teacher AI adoption survey (schools in India). See all ongoing research.

Before you write

Questions people ask

Who are your workshops for?

Classroom teachers, school leaders, teacher educators and education authorities. Content is adapted to the audience: hands-on practice for teachers, strategy and governance for leaders.

Are the workshops about a particular AI tool?

No. I use widely available tools for hands-on practice, but the focus is judgement: understanding how AI works, using it well, and checking what it produces. Those skills transfer as tools change.

Do I need to take part in research to book a workshop?

No. Research participation is always optional, anonymous and based on consent. Many schools choose to take part because they receive evidence about their own staff.

Do you work outside India and Finland?

Yes. India and Finland are my main research sites, but the research questions are global, and I welcome enquiries from any education system.

Is your research peer-reviewed?

Yes. My work is published in peer-reviewed journals such as Artificial Intelligence Review and Discover Education and in peer-reviewed conference proceedings, including HICSS and IEEE conferences. See the publications.

Can researchers use aittt.app?

Yes. The instrument is freely available to the research community. Please get in touch.
